Privacy Policy

How CLIQ handles customer information for ordering, account access, and cafe operations.

Table of Contents

1. Information We Collect

When you use our app, we may collect personal details such as your name, email address, and phone number to process orders. We also record order information, including items purchased, order history, payment method, and special instructions. Full payment card information is not stored by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 To process and confirm customer orders.
2.2 To contact users regarding order updates or issues.
2.3 To maintain accurate records for sales and operational reporting.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us process payments, manage orders, and support app operations. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our business, customers, or others.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to store and protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No online transmission or storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to personal information we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate information, or ask us to delete data when it is no longer necessary for our services. You may contact us to exercise these rights.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may revise or update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in practices, legal requirements, or operational needs. Updates will be posted in the app, and continued use of the app signifies acceptance of the revised policy.

7. User Responsibilities

7.1 Provide accurate and updated information.
7.2 Keep login credentials secure and confidential.
7.3 Log out after each session to prevent unauthorized access.
